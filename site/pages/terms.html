2. What the service is

LLMJob pools spare GPU capacity into an OpenAI-compatible inference network. In practice that means four things:

  • Chat — a free assistant on the website, subject to a usage allowance.
  • The API — an OpenAI-compatible endpoint authenticated with an LLMJob API key. See the documentation.
  • The node network — machines that people connect to serve inference jobs.
  • LLMJob Earn — a desktop app that puts idle GPU time to work.

Requests may be served either by the node network or, for the models marked as hosted in the documentation, by an upstream provider we route to on your behalf. Which one served a given request is reported in the response.

3. API keys

Your API key authenticates requests and consumes resources, so treat it as a secret. We store only a hash of it — we cannot recover a lost key, and you should not ask us to. Keep keys out of browser and mobile code, where anyone can read them.

You are responsible for all usage on your keys, including usage by someone who obtained a key you failed to protect. Revoke a compromised key from the dashboard; revocation takes effect immediately. We may revoke or rate-limit a key that is being abused, or that threatens the stability of the network.

A key set to private routes only to nodes in your own account. A key set to public may be served by any node on the network, and may use the hosted models. Choose deliberately: the routing setting is how you decide where your prompts are allowed to run.

4. Running a node

When you connect a machine you are volunteering it to the network. You confirm that you own the machine or are authorised to use it this way, and that doing so doesn't breach anyone else's rules — an employer's, a landlord's, a hosting provider's, or an electricity supplier's.

If your node is public, inference jobs submitted by other users may run on it, and prompts you did not write will pass through your hardware. If that isn't what you want, keep the node private. Either way you remain responsible for your own machine: its power use, its wear, its cooling, its network, and its security.

We may remove a node from the network at any time — for unreliability, for abuse, or because it is failing jobs. Nothing here entitles you to have work sent to your node, or to any particular volume of it.

5. LLMJob Earn and payouts

LLMJob Earn mines cryptocurrency using a third-party mining pool. Some things follow from that, and they matter:

  • We do not custody your funds. Rewards are calculated and paid by the pool directly to the payout address you supply. We never hold, forward or control them.
  • The payout address is yours to get right. Cryptocurrency transactions are irreversible. If you enter an address you do not control, or one with a typo, the funds are gone and we cannot recover them.
  • Earnings are not guaranteed or predicted. They depend on your hardware, network difficulty, pool rules, uptime and the token's value — none of which we control. Any figure shown in the app or on the site is an estimate, not a promise.
  • Pool terms apply too. Payout schedules, minimum thresholds and fees are set by the pool, not by us, and can change without notice to us.
  • Tax is your responsibility. Mining rewards may be taxable where you live.

Mining draws sustained power and runs your hardware hard. Satisfy yourself that this is appropriate for your machine and your electricity costs before you start.
