Guide

Run an LLM on your HiveOS rig

The LLMJob Earn miner can run a local AI model on your GPUs while it mines. It's off by default on HiveOS. Turning it on takes one line.

1
Open your flight sheet

The one with the llmjob-earn miner (set up on the download page).

2
Add one flag

In the miner's settings:

Extra config arguments
--mode auto
3
Apply and check the log

The model downloads once (about 5 GB). Then:

local LLM ready — OpenAI endpoint http://127.0.0.1:8080/v1
⛏ Mining
→prompt arrives
💬 Answering
→10 s after the last one
⛏ Mining

Only that card pauses. Your other cards keep mining.

Use the model

It speaks the OpenAI API. On the rig:

http://127.0.0.1:8080/v1

With --mode llm, other computers on your network can use http://<rig IP>:8000/v1.

Handy options

  • --llm-max-instances 1: one card only. Good for small CPUs like Octominers.
  • --mode llm: model only, no mining.
  • --mode mining: back to mining only.
~6 GB free GPU memory per card ~5 GB disk for the model Best tested on NVIDIA No extra earnings yet
Port 8000 has no password. Keep it on your home network and don't forward it to the internet. Questions? Ask in our Discord.
