How we test

  • Same PC, taking turns. Each card is rented once (on Salad, Vast or Clore) and every miner runs on it in turn, so the GPU, driver, power limit and cooling are the same for all of them.
  • Five minutes each, after a warm-up. We average the hashrate each miner prints over the whole run. Earlier figures were 5-minute runs too.
  • Reported hashrate is honest. We checked: on a test pool that proves every share, each miner's reported rate matched the work it actually did to within its dev fee (ours +0.6%, PeakMiner −3.0%, SRBMiner −1.0%, OneZeroMiner −0.1% over 15-minute runs).
  • Fees come off first. With "After dev fees" on, as it starts, the table shows what reaches your wallet: PeakMiner and SRBMiner keep 2%, OneZeroMiner 1%, LLMJob Earn 0%. Switch it off for raw hashrate.
  • Stock settings. No overclocks or memory-clock changes, for any miner.
  • Open data. Every run, with the PC and versions, is in benchmark.md on GitHub.
