(Vast vmem_usage), so no other tenant. `--mine-mem-clock` can't take effect here (the miner
log says it has no permission to set clocks), as on every Vast box so far.

575 W, at 67-74 C; g0 sits at 2137 MHz, 368 W, 62 C. Their max SM clock is 3090 MHz (g0:
3210). So something other than power or heat holds the clock, most likely a clock limit
set on the host. On a card held by its clock, saving DRAM watts can't raise the clock the
way it did on the power-capped Ada and small Blackwell cards.
