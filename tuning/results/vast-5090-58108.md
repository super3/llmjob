Driver 580.126.09, compute 12.0, power limit 575 W on every card, memory 13801 MHz.
Re-rented 10:20 UTC Oct 8 on box.sh c835a90, so builds can use CUDA 13.3 ("cuda": "13").
The release default on this card is the CUDA 13 core. VRAM in use 0.5 GB before mining
(Vast vmem_usage), so no other tenant. `--mine-mem-clock` can't take effect here (the miner
log says it has no permission to set clocks), as on every Vast box so far.

The cards are not at their power limit. g1-g3 sit at 2242-2250 MHz drawing 449-477 W of
575 W, at 67-74 C; g0 sits at 2137 MHz, 368 W, 62 C. Their max SM clock is 3090 MHz (g0:
3210). So something other than power or heat holds the clock, most likely a clock limit
set on the host. On a card held by its clock, saving DRAM watts can't raise the clock the
way it did on the power-capped Ada and small Blackwell cards.

## Where it stands (11:45 UTC)

The box was outbid at 11:00 and released at 11:28. Since about 11:17 Vast refuses every
re-rent and bid change on the account with "insufficient_credit", so it can't come back
until the account has credit. The control file is back on the release for every GPU. The
three CUDA 13.3 builds stay listed, so a re-rented box builds them and a later agent can
finish test 2 straight away: g1 `build:cb1024`, g2 `build:serp`, g0 and g3 as control.

On this host the 5090s are held near 2242 MHz well under their 575 W limit, so a change that
saves DRAM watts has nothing to give back to the clock. Expect col_batch 1024 and the
serpentine walk to stay level here. A 5090 held at a 400-450 W cap (59404, 151626) is the
better place to test them.

No verified gain on this box, so nothing was rolled out.
