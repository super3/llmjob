# vast-4090-151594: RTX 4090 x1, Japan (Vast machine 151594, spot)

Ada, compute 8.9, driver 580.178.04, 500 W power limit, max SM clock 3150 MHz. v0.5.12
release, one miner. The release core on Ada is cu12.

This card is not at its power limit: it draws about 392-398 W of its 500 W at 2415-2475 MHz
and 76-77 C. So the clock is set by something other than power (heat, voltage, or a host
clock limit we can't see from the container). Saving energy per hash should matter less here
than on a power-capped card; work per clock is what counts.

## Baseline (release default = cu12 core)

| GPU | TH/s (mean) | Window (UTC, Oct 8) | sm / mem MHz, W, C |
|---|---|---|---|
| g0 | 305.2 (n=5, 303.5-307.0) | 08:43:06-08:46:09 and 08:57:31 | 2415 / 10251, 392/500 W, 76 C |

Spot pauses cut the window short: the box was outbid at 08:48 and resumed at 08:55, and the
first test went in 2 minutes after that. Before the move to `tuning/box.sh` at 08:40, the old
script read 318-319 TH/s on this machine for 40 minutes. The new rental may have landed on a
different physical card of the machine; the cause isn't known.
