# vast-4070super-116195: RTX 4070 Super x4, United States (Vast machine 116195, host 410852, spot)

Ada, compute 8.9, driver 580.142. Power limit 160 W per card (stock is 220 W), max SM clock
3105-3120 MHz, 48 MB L2. v0.5.12 release, one miner per GPU. On Ada the release loads the
CUDA 12.8 core (cu13 is picked on its own only for compute 12.x).

All four cards sit at the power cap: W=159.8/160, util 100%. The SM clock is what moves,
and the hottest card (g3, 84-86 C) clocks lowest. Memory stays at 10251 MHz, and Vast
containers can't lock it lower.

card the rule runs col_batch 1024 instead of 2048, so a launch's B' fits the 48 MB L2.

Notes:

- Test 1 ran across a spot pause: 5 readings at 09:13-09:17 (g2 -0.19%, g3 +0.19%) and 14
  after the box resumed on the same settings. Both under 1%.
- Why col_batch helps: at 2048 a launch sweeps 64 MB of B' plus a 6 MB band of A', which a
  48 MB L2 can't hold, so B' comes back from DRAM once a band (43 times a launch). At 1024 it
  is 32 MB and stays in the L2; band 64 reads it 11 times instead. The card is at its power
  cap, so the DRAM watts saved go to the SM clock. An LRU model of the walk put the DRAM
  saving at only ~11 GB/s and predicted under 0.5%; the card gave 2.5-3%, so the real L2 does
  worse at 2048 than LRU would.
- Builds that need the hit check each cost a reboot on this old-script box when two are
  switched at once (the watcher can reboot between them). Switch one build GPU per round, or
  put it first in GPU order, so all other switches land right after its check.
