3105-3120 MHz, 48 MB L2. v0.5.12 release, one miner per GPU. On Ada the release loads the

and the hottest card clocks lowest. Memory stays at 10251 MHz.

Rollout check, same window (10:05-10:15), each GPU on cb1024 against its own release mean
from 09:27-09:40: g1 113.52 (+2.0%), g2 116.69 (+2.6%), g3 112.09 (+2.7%). 0 invalid shares
on every GPU. SM clocks at 10:08: g1 2055, g2 2100, g3 2055 MHz, all at 159.8-159.9 W.

Test 2 ran after two reboots (one per hit check); the window starts 2 minutes after the
last resume (09:48:59). Both changes cut how often B' comes back from DRAM. At 2048 a launch
sweeps 64 MB of B', plus a 6 MB band of A', which a 48 MB L2 can't hold, so B' is read
from DRAM once per band (43 times a launch). At 1024 it is 32 MB and stays in the L2;
band 64 reads it 11 times. The card is at its power cap, so the DRAM watts saved go to the
SM clock.

Test 1 ran across a spot pause: 5 readings at 09:13-09:17 (g2 -0.19%, g3 +0.19%) and 10
readings at 09:27-09:36 after the box resumed on the same settings. Both under 1%.
