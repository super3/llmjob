memory 13801 MHz. Every card sits at the 400 W cap, so the box is power-bound: g2 and g3 run
at a lower SM clock than g0 and g1 at the same watts. VRAM in use ~1.8 GB, so no other tenant.

09:22. `--mine-mem-clock` can't take effect here (no permission to set clocks), as elsewhere.
