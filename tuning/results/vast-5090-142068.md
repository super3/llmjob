## Diagnosis: the GPU is shared with something outside our container

The card itself looks healthy:

- Power: full 575 W limit, drawing 574 W. Not a low power limit.
- Clocks: SM 2497 MHz, higher than Maryland's 2145 MHz at 450 W. Not throttled.
- Temperature: 71 C. Not thermal throttling.
- PCIe 4.0 x16. Mining doesn't depend on PCIe anyway.
- Memory clock 13801 MHz: the 7001 MHz lock did not take effect, but it doesn't on any
  Vast box (Maryland shows the same), so it can't explain a 2x gap.

What is wrong is VRAM. The GPU had **7.4 GB in use** (Vast's `vmem_usage` 7.39 GB, and our
network board row 7074 MB). Every other 5090 running the same miner shows 1.4-1.8 GB
(Maryland 1.85, Alberta 1.50, Vietnam 1.85 in Vast; Maryland 1396 MB and Ontario 1395 MB on
the board). So about 5.5 GB belonged to another process outside our container.

That fits the numbers. The card ran at full power, a high clock and 100% utilization, but
our miner got 186 TH/s. Scaled from Maryland by SM clock, 2497 MHz should give about 410.
We got 45% of that, which is what you'd see if two compute jobs were time-slicing one GPU.
Our own old instance (54810109, worker `vast-5090-142068`) was not the other job: the pool
had no share from it after about 08:38.

Not proven: the planned check (force the cu12 core, so only one CUDA runtime is loaded, and
see whether VRAM stays near 7 GB) was pushed at 08:52 but the box was destroyed before it
picked it up. The 7.4 GB from Vast's own host-side reading is still strong evidence on its
own.

No setting on our side can fix a second tenant on the GPU.

## What this means for picking hosts

- Skip machine 142068. Vast's listing gave no warning: it showed a normal 5090 (575 W,
  PCIe 4.0 x16, 99.6% reliability, verified).
- After any new rental, check VRAM in use within the first few minutes, from Vast's
  `vmem_usage` or the board's `vramUsedMb`. A 5090 running only our miner uses under 2 GB.
  Much more than that, together with low TH/s at full power, means the GPU is shared.
- Vast's `gpu_mem_bw` and `dlperf` don't flag this. This box showed 716 GB/s and 97, but so
  does Alberta (721, 100), the fastest 5090 we have.
- Host 410852 also runs our slow 4070 Super boxes (about 110 TH/s per GPU against 141 in
  Ontario). Its 4070S box 116195 showed 1.45 GB VRAM in Vast, which looks normal, so that gap
  may have another cause. Worth checking the same way before trusting this host.
